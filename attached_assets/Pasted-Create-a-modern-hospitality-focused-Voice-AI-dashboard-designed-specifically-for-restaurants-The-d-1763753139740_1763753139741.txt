Create a modern, hospitality-focused Voice-AI dashboard designed specifically for restaurants. The design should feel warm, approachable, and service-oriented, while still maintaining a professional, tech-driven aesthetic.

Overall Look & Feel

Light, warm, friendly interface inspired by restaurant ambiance (soft lighting, warm tones).

Background is a gentle cream or parchment-like beige, giving a hospitality vibe rather than a fintech/enterprise feel.

All main panels appear as floating cards with rounded corners and soft shadows, resembling menus or tabletop placards.

Visual tone should feel premium but comfortable, not sterile or overly corporate.

Purpose & Identity

This dashboard is for:

Managing restaurant Voice-AI agents (reservation handling, phone answering, order intake, table management).

Reviewing call transcripts, agent performance, customer sentiment, and booking outcomes.

Providing accessible insights for busy restaurant operators.

The aesthetic should feel like:
“Modern AI meets upscale restaurant UI.”

Layout

Top navigation bar with logo, menu items (Home, Agents, Reservations, Settings), and a search field for calls or customers.

Main dashboard (left):
Analytics cards showing call volume, reservation conversions, missed call prevention, customer sentiment, average response time, etc.

Right sidebar:
Restaurant manager profile, restaurant location details, and high-level status indicators (AI online/offline, reservation load, peak hours).

Modular card grid, arranged in 2–3 rows:

Call volume timeline

AI-handled vs. staff-handled calls

Reservation summary

Customer sentiment chart

Popular call intents (e.g., Waitlist, Hours, Menu Requests, Catering)

Voice-agent performance metrics

Color Palette

Shift away from fintech colors and adopt warm, restaurant-friendly tones:

Background: Soft cream, parchment, warm beige (#FFF6E8 style).

Primary accent: Deep green / forest green (#0E4F4F), reminiscent of upscale restaurant branding.

Secondary accent: Warm golden yellow (#FFC857), evoking hospitality and warmth.

Card backgrounds: Clean off-white.

Text: Charcoal for headers, soft gray for secondary labels.

Optional accent: A muted terracotta or wine red for small highlights (e.g., peak times, busy warnings).

Typography

Use a soft, modern sans-serif with hospitality friendliness:

Fonts like Poppins, Nunito, or Inter.

Headers: Medium–bold, warm, slightly larger.

Labels / secondary text: Friendly, small and readable.

Big numbers (KPIs): Bold, clean, slightly rounded.

Component Style

Cards:

Rounded corners (18–24px)

Subtle shadows

Balanced padding, airy spacing

Profile section:
Humanizing—photo, restaurant name, manager name.

Buttons & toggles:
Rounded, pill-shaped, using green or yellow accents.

Tags / badges:
Small rounded pills for statuses like Peak Hours, AI Live, After-Hours Calls, New Reservation.

Icons:
Minimal line icons: headset, phone, table, reservation book, microphone, bell, chef hat.

Charts

Charts should be simple and welcoming:

Call volume line graph in warm golden yellow.

Reservation conversions with green highlights.

Sentiment charts using soft greens (positive), neutrals (neutral), and warm terracotta (negative).

Popular call topics as rounded bar charts or pill clusters.

Avoid harsh gridlines; keep visuals airy and friendly.

Interaction Feel

Hover states gently raise cards with a slightly stronger shadow.

Buttons deepen in color on hover.

Interface should feel smooth, intuitive, and easy for a restaurant manager to use quickly during rush hours.
